Upload attendance

HR cannot always mark everyone's attendance by hand — so upload your own days from a sheet instead.
Prepare a file with two columns, Date and Status (Present, Late, Half Day or Absent) — or start from the sample template (CSV). You can fill the sheet in Excel and upload it as .xlsx or .csv — both work. The rows are recorded for your own account only. A date that already has a record is updated with the new status.
